How to read it. Stocks are the S&P 500, 400 and 600. A result counts when earnings grew (revenue and net income against the same quarter last year) and the price reacted well. The entry, the stop (1% under the result-day low, judged on the close), the targets and the grade follow the same rules as the Indian page. Target 1 is +1R: on a close above it the stop moves to cost. Book half on a close above Target 2 (+2R) and trail the rest on the 21-day EMA.

Data. Result dates and EPS from Nasdaq's earnings calendar; prices and quarterly figures from Yahoo. Growth figures can take a few days to appear after a result; until then the stock shows "growth pending" and no levels. A dash means the value is not available. Prices are daily closes, not live.

A mechanical screen for study, not investment advice.
