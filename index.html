Chethan's 6-Gate Entry & Exit Rules

1. Eligibility

Market Cap & Timing

Mcap > ₹1,000 Cr on NSE/BSE. Zero pre-result entries. Pre-move test: flat base passes; rally >15% triggers "Priced In" wait.

2. When to Enter

RDH Reclaim + 2x Volume

Enter only on a decisive close/reclaim above the Result-Day High (RDH) on volume >2x the 20-DMA volume.

3. Stop Loss (SL)

Reaction Day Low (≤4-5%)

Hard SL anchored at the post-result reaction candle low. Risk capped: (Entry − SL) / Entry × 100 ≤ 4% to 5%.

4. Trailing SL (TSL)

10-EMA & 20-DMA Trail

Move SL to Breakeven at +1R (+5% gain). Trail tactical swings on the 10-EMA. Trail multi-quarter J-curves along the 20-DMA.

5. How Much to Put

Allocation Ladder (10% → 30%)

0% while unconfirmed; 10% on initial RDH breakout; 20% & 30% only as secondary consolidation bases validate. Max 1% portfolio risk.

6. Sector Tailwind

Above 10, 20, 50 & 200 DMA

Tailwind awarded strictly when sector index is above 10, 20, 50, and 200 DMA. Otherwise Neutral / Unrated.

Why Pre-Earnings Stocks Cannot Be Qualified:

PEAD stands for Post-Earnings Announcement Drift. Entering or declaring qualification prior to results is speculative anticipation. A stock only qualifies when the earnings print is strong and institutional volume reclaims the Result-Day High.
